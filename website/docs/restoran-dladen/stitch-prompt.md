## Prompt (paste as-is)

```
Design a modern one-page website for "Restoran D'LaDen", a traditional Malaysian
kampung (village) restaurant in Bandar Seri Putra, Bangi, Selangor.

STYLE DIRECTION — "modern kampung warm":
- Warm ivory/cream background (#FBF6EC), terracotta (#C1502E) primary, deep leaf
  green (#2E5339) secondary, soft gold accent (#D9A441)
- Subtle traditional Malay songket textile motif as thin decorative borders and
  section dividers (geometric diamond pattern, low opacity)
- Rounded-corner cards, soft warm shadows, generous whitespace
- Typography: friendly serif display for headlines (e.g. Fraunces/Playfair style),
  clean humanist sans for body
- Mobile-first, airy, appetizing, professional but homey — NOT luxury fine dining,
  NOT garish festive. Think "beautiful village kitchen".

SECTIONS (top to bottom, one-page with sticky header):

1. HEADER (sticky): wordmark "Restoran D'LaDen" left; nav links: Utama, Menu,
   Pesanan Pukal, Lokasi; terracotta pill button "Pesan Sekarang".

2. HERO: split layout — left: eyebrow "Bandar Seri Putra, Bangi", big headline
   "Rendang Tok Warisan, Sarapan Kampung Setiap Pagi", subtext about nasi lemak,
   pulut kuning and rendang cooked with authentic village recipes, two CTAs
   (solid terracotta "Pesan Catering / Rendang Pukal" + outlined WhatsApp button
   with phone 012-246 6678), small badge "Buka setiap hari · 6:30 pagi – 4:00 petang".
   Right: appetizing hero photo of Malaysian rendang with rice in rustic clay
   pottery, warm morning light.

3. MENU GRID ("Menu Kegemaran"): 6 cards, each with food photo, name, price, and
   one-line description:
   - Rendang Tok — RM55/500g · RM100/1kg — slow-cooked rich dry rendang, signature
   - Pulut Kuning Rendang — from RM16 — yellow glutinous rice with rendang, wedding
     morning classic
   - Nasi Lemak & Lauk Pagi — from RM8 — fried spiced chicken, sambal everyone returns for
   - Kuih Muih Tradisional — from RM4.90 — colorful traditional Malay kuih assortment
   - Sambal Krupkrap (Botol) — RM20 — crispy sambal bilis/paru in a jar
   - Kari Kambing & Gulai Kawah — from RM15 — big-pot curry, hearty breakfast or events

4. BULK & CATERING ("Kenduri, Mesyuarat & Stok Rumah — Kami Sedia"): full-width warm
   green band with cream text, 3 feature columns (Rendang & daging pukal 500g/1kg,
   Whole chicken rendang & masak merah, Kari kambing & gulai kawah 1kg), note
   "Tempah 2–3 hari awal untuk majlis besar", CTA button to order form.

5. TESTIMONIALS ("Kata Mereka"): 3 simple quote cards with reviewer first names
   (Iza, Intan, Wati), 5 gold stars each, short Malay quotes.

6. ORDER FORM ("Pesan Sekarang"): two-column — left: reassurance text "isi borang,
   kami WhatsApp anda untuk sahkan pesanan dan harga" plus phone/WhatsApp; right:
   clean form card with fields Nama Penuh, No. Telefon, Jenis Pesanan (dropdown),
   Tarikh Diperlukan (date), Kuantiti/Pax, Butiran Pesanan (textarea), solid
   terracotta submit button "Hantar Pesanan".

7. LOCATION ("Datang & Rasa Sendiri"): Google Maps embed placeholder on one side,
   address "No 24, Jalan Seri Putra 3/13, Bandar Seri Putra, 43000 Bangi, Selangor",
   opening hours, Foodpanda badge, phone.

8. FOOTER: deep green, wordmark, one-line description in Malay, contact column,
   copyright "© 2026 Restoran D'LaDen".

Output: full web design (desktop + mobile), all text in Malay (Bahasa Malaysia),
photorealistic appetizing Malaysian food photography style.
```

- Kalau Stitch output pelik, pecahkan prompt ikut section dan generate satu-satu

- Gambar makanan: boleh minta ChatGPT generate terus per-menu item (rendang tok, pulut kuning, nasi lemak, kuih-muih, sambal botol, kari kambing)
